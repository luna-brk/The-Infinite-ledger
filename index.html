By Order of the Merkhant Consortium · Sigil · MMXXVI

The Infinite Ledger

A Prospectus & Standing Index

Prepared for the guild-brothers & sisters of the Cage,
and for such licensed correspondents as continue to keep our standards.

It is the quiet satisfaction of the Consortium that, in the two ages since our founding, no planar market of consequence has moved without our consultation, and few have moved against our counsel for very long. This volume is offered in that same spirit of consultation: a standing record of the rates, routes, and rights by which lawful trade passes between the planes — gathered here not as proclamation, but as a courtesy to the prudent cutter who would prefer to know the terms before he learns them.

We do not set the price of the multiverse. We merely keep the scales honest, the contracts sealed, and the belief-token redeemable at a figure the Lady's city can live with. Arbitrage is a respectable craft and remains permitted. Fraud is not. Those who cannot observe the distinction tend, in our experience, to find other employment — or other planes. The Ledger, at no cost to the reader, is the most reliable guide to which is which.

◆The Three Pillars◆

Coherence

A price the Consortium posts is a price the Consortium stands behind. That it matches the price on the Plane of Fire is not coincidence; it is practice.

PF1e Compatibility

Wealth-By-Level is the structural spine of the trade. The Ledger gives it context, and occasionally a tighter waist. It does not overrule it.

Merchant Fascination

Complexity is offered, never pressed. A cutter who wants a sword buys a sword. The speculator reads further, and is welcomed — on our terms.
