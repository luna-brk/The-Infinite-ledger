The Infinite Ledger

Master Index & Navigation

The economy is a toy, not a tax. Players should want to arbitrage soul shells between the Grey Waste and Bytopia — to argue whether to pay the Merkhant Consortium in belief-tokens or raw quintessence. The complexity is opt-in: a player who just wants to buy a sword can buy a sword.

Coherence

Every price must survive the question: "but why can't I just buy that on the Plane of Fire?"

PF1e Compatibility

Wealth-By-Level is the structural spine. This system contextualizes it — never violates it.

Player Fascination
