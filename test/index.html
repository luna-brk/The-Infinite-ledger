§ 1.4 Advanced Concepts ▾

This section explores more complex topics related to planar economics and trade.

Subsection A: Deep Dive

Understanding the nuances of cross-planar commerce requires careful study of how different planes interact economically.

The fundamental principle is that no economy exists in isolation. Every transaction ripples across the multiverse.

Subsection B: Advanced Applications

Once you've mastered the basics, you can begin to see opportunities for arbitrage and sophisticated trading strategies.

§ 1.6 Common Misconceptions ▾

New GMs often stumble over these common pitfalls when implementing the Ledger system.

Myth: "Gp is just a number"

False. In this system, gp represents narrative weight and player agency. A player who carefully manages their wealth has a richer game experience than one who ignores economics.

Myth: "Planar arbitrage will break my game"

Only if you let it. The anti-exploit toolkit in Module VIII gives you complete control over the economy. Use it.

Myth: "Players don't care about economics"

Some don't. Many do. By making economics optional but rewarding, you give all players agency without forcing complexity on anyone.
